## Essential Tools for Electrical Engineers & EV Owners

All calculators run **100% client-side** — your data never leaves your browser, ideal for sensitive electrical scenarios.

### EV Charging
- [Charging Time Calculator](/tools/charging-time-calculator/) — Enter battery kWh + power kW, instantly calculate 0→80%, 0→90%, 0→100% charging duration. Covers 7 kW to 250 kW.
- [Charging Power Calculator](/tools/charging-power-calculator/) — Enter V/A/cosφ, get active kW / reactive kVar / apparent kVA. Supports single-phase 220 V and three-phase 380 V.

### Power Distribution & Engineering
- [Three-Phase Power Calculator](/tools/three-phase-power-calculator/) — Enter line voltage/current/power factor, instantly get active/apparent/reactive power with compensation recommendations.
- [Circuit Breaker Size Calculator](/tools/circuit-breaker-sizing-calculator/) — NEC 240/430 OCPD sizing with EV charger, motor, and subpanel presets.
- [Wire Size Calculator](/tools/wire-size-calculator/) — NEC 310.16 AWG/kcmil sizing with voltage drop verification and next-standard OCPD.
- [Cable Ampacity Lookup](/tools/cable-current-carrying-capacity-lookup/) — YJV/YJV22/VV online lookup, covering 5 installation methods per GB 50217.
- [Transformer Capacity Selection](/tools/transformer-capacity-selection/) — kVA sizing per GB/T 17468 R10, supporting single/multi-device and motor inrush modes.
- [Electricity Cost Estimator](/tools/electricity-cost-calculator/) — Enter appliance wattage + daily hours + rate, get daily/monthly/annual cost and carbon estimate.
